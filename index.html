Version 1.10.5 — connect the board via USB and click below.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off. Erasing wipes WiFi, city, leagues, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

New since 1.9.4

  • WiFi supervises itself. The firmware used to connect exactly once at boot and never again — a lost lease or a router reboot meant a power cycle. It now re-associates on its own, and only counts as connected when it actually holds an address (a link reporting 0.0.0.0 used to look healthy while the settings page and every feed quietly failed).
  • The day boundary is noticed. At local midnight the retired-row count clears and every feed refetches under the new date, instead of everything keeping yesterday's meaning until a restart.
  • A failed sports fetch keeps the last good scores rather than committing an empty list and clearing the tab.
  • The clock is local from the first frame. The saved zone is installed before anything reads localtime(), so no UTC flash — and the night window can no longer compare a UTC hour against your dimming hours and dim the panel at boot.
  • Finished calendar rows retire on the minute; all-day and past-midnight entries never do, and the tab says "N earlier" instead of claiming the day is empty.
  • Sports parsing handles full 30–40 game days: 64 games, 50 per league, and no more dropped games from a buffer that compacted mid-block.
  • Settings page renders from the saved config, so revisiting it no longer resets theme and leagues; sections collapse; fetch-now and per-feed test are there for checking a URL.
  • Rank gutters on score rows for ranked matchups.

What to check after installing
  • It boots repeatedly — restart it two or three times, not just once.
  • Header clock is local immediately; panel does not dim at boot unless it is genuinely inside your night window.
  • Sports, calendar, stocks and weather all populate.
  • Reboot your router once: the device should return on its own within about a minute.
  • Leave it running across midnight and check the morning.

If something is wrong, 1.10.1 (last build confirmed on hardware) and 1.9.4 (previous stable) are both still installable.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.
