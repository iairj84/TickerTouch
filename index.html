TickerTouch

Version 1.10.9 — connect the board via USB and click below.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off. Erasing wipes WiFi, city, leagues, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

New in 1.10.9 — stocks was never being fetched, and sports wasted a minute. The fetch loop handles one feed per call and returns after each, and it stamped a feed's timer on the way into the fetch. That measures start-to-start, so a sports pass taking about 66 seconds across five leagues against a 60-second interval was due again the instant it finished: it re-entered at the top of the queue every time and the stocks check below it was never reached. Finance sat at zero items with no [Stocks] line in the log at all — not a refusal, a fetch that never happened.

Timers are now stamped after the fetch returns, so a slow feed yields to the ones behind it. The sports parser was also spinning to its per-league 12-second deadline after each body had already ended, which is why a five-league pass took a full minute; it now stops as soon as the connection closes with nothing left to parse.

Finance asks once, and has a second source (1.10.8). The whole symbol list goes out in a single request instead of one per symbol, and if that comes back with no prices at all the board asks CNBC — a different host on a different edge — in one request of its own. The two are never mixed, and serial tags a fallback price with (cnbc).

Fixed in 1.10.6 — data stopped at midnight. 1.10.5 added a day-rollover check that compared today against the last day it had seen, then returned before recording the new one. The comparison stayed true, so from local midnight onward every tick re-armed the feed timers and returned without fetching: scores froze on yesterday's FINAL, the calendar kept saying Today with yesterday's date, and every feed on /diag sat on waiting until a reboot. The clock and settings page kept working the whole time, which is what made it look like a network problem. 1.10.6 records the new day before refreshing, so the boundary fires once and fetching continues.

New since 1.9.4

  • The fetch schedule can no longer starve a feed. Intervals are measured from when a fetch finishes, so a slow one yields instead of monopolising the loop — the defect that left Finance permanently empty.
  • Sports finishes promptly. A league stops parsing when its body ends rather than idling to a timeout.
  • Finance has two providers and costs one request per cycle.
  • WiFi supervises itself. The firmware used to connect exactly once at boot and never again — a lost lease or a router reboot meant a power cycle. It now re-associates on its own, and only counts as connected when it actually holds an address (a link reporting 0.0.0.0 used to look healthy while the settings page and every feed quietly failed).
  • The day boundary is noticed — properly. At local midnight the retired-row count clears and every feed refetches under the new date, instead of everything keeping yesterday's meaning until a restart. (The first attempt at this, in 1.10.5, wedged the fetch loop at midnight; 1.10.6 is the corrected version.)
  • A failed sports fetch keeps the last good scores rather than committing an empty list and clearing the tab.
  • The clock is local from the first frame. The saved zone is installed before anything reads localtime(), so no UTC flash — and the night window can no longer compare a UTC hour against your dimming hours and dim the panel at boot.
  • Finished calendar rows retire on the minute; all-day and past-midnight entries never do, and the tab says "N earlier" instead of claiming the day is empty.
  • Sports parsing handles full 30–40 game days: 64 games, 50 per league, and no more dropped games from a buffer that compacted mid-block.
  • Settings page renders from the saved config, so revisiting it no longer resets theme and leagues; sections collapse; fetch-now and per-feed test are there for checking a URL.
  • Rank gutters on score rows for ranked matchups.

What to check after installing
  • [Stocks] lines appear within about thirty seconds of boot — this is the fix to watch. Four of them, or three plus a no data line, and (cnbc) after a price if the first provider refused.
  • [Sports] total=N follows the last league almost immediately instead of a minute later.
  • It boots repeatedly — restart it two or three times, not just once.
  • Header clock is local immediately; panel does not dim at boot unless it is genuinely inside your night window.
  • Reboot your router once: the device should return on its own within about a minute.
  • Leave it running across midnight. In the morning /diag should show every feed with a recent age and no waiting, the calendar header should name the actual date, and serial should show one [Data] new day line rather than one a second.

If something is wrong: 1.10.8 (previous stable, Finance still starved), 1.10.7, 1.10.6 (midnight fix, before the Finance change), 1.10.5 (fetches stall at midnight), 1.10.1 (last build confirmed on hardware) and 1.9.4 (earlier stable) are all still installable.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.
