TickerTouch Firmware Installer

Version 1.7.2 — connect the board via USB and click below.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device before flashing. Leave erase off — erasing wipes the WiFi credentials, city and league selection, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop.

This build carries the calendar work: two feeds with colour and icon badges, events converted using the timezone each feed declares, and an hour that no longer drifts across a daylight-saving change.
