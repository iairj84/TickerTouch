TickerTouch Firmware Installer

Version 1.9.4 — connect the board via USB and click below.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device before flashing. Leave erase off — erasing wipes the WiFi credentials, city and league selection, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop.

This build carries everything since 1.7.2. Settings can be exported and restored as a file, and the panel dims itself at night at the hours you choose. Sections collapse to headings so thirty fields stop being one wall, and each calendar, the tickers and the crypto list have a Fetch now button that reports what was actually parsed.

The Calendar tab and the ticker now drop events once they finish, reading the end time from each feed, and say Today - 2 earlier, nothing left rather than claiming the day was empty.

Settings backup is verified on hardware; restore has not been exercised on a device yet, so keep the file you export.

Test builds are published at /beta/.
