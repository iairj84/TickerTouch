TickerTouch — Finished Events Drop Off

Version 1.9.1 — beta. The default installer is 1.7.2.

Today now means what is still to come. Once an event's end time passes, it leaves the Calendar tab and the ticker on the minute it finishes, instead of sitting there until midnight. A 9:00 standup at 1:00 PM is gone.

The end comes from the feed's DTEND, or its DURATION when that is what it sends. An entry with neither is assumed to last an hour — the spec says an instant, but disappearing the moment a meeting starts would look like a bug. All-day entries and anything running past midnight stay put until the day ends, and later days are untouched.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off — erasing wipes WiFi, city, leagues, both calendar URLs and the icon choices. Nothing in this build changes the saved settings format.

What to check after installing
  • The Calendar tab shows only events that have not finished yet today.
  • The ticker's calendar segment loses the same rows.
  • Tomorrow's events are all still listed.
  • An all-day entry stays visible for the whole day.
  • Calendar → Fetch now agrees with the tab: a finished meeting does not come back after a refresh.

A row disappears only when the feed states an end on the same day. Times are compared in local time, using the zone each event was written in.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.
