TickerTouch — Room For A Full Slate

Version 1.9.6 — beta. Stable is 1.9.4.

A busy multi-league day now fits. Three caps were in the way, and only one of them was where you would look for it.

32 games total, across every selected league, was the hard ceiling — and once it filled, the remaining leagues were never even requested. That's now 64. 25 games per league was ESPN's answer because that is what the request asked for; it now asks for 50. And the ticker cut the scoreboard off at 512 characters, about eight games, so a full slate looked truncated even once the data was there; a segment now holds 3072 characters and the assembled line 8192.

The per-league deadline went from 8 s to 12 s so a 50-game response is not cut off mid-stream, and hitting the 64-game cap now prints a line instead of dropping leagues in silence.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off — erasing wipes WiFi, city, leagues, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

What to check after installing
  • The Sports tab lists every game on a busy day, across leagues, without a cut.
  • The ticker's sports segment runs to the end of the slate rather than stopping mid-league.
  • Serial shows [Sports] total=N with N above 32 when the day warrants it.
  • No capped at 64 line on a normal day.

This costs about 28 KB of static RAM (64 score entries, larger ticker buffers) — the build reports roughly 42% of the 327 KB, with the ticker text itself coming from PSRAM.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.
