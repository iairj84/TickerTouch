Version 1.5.2 — this is not the default firmware.

Fixes the duplicated calendar entries introduced by 1.5.1.

A calendar feed delivers a repeating event once, and then delivers another copy of any single occurrence that was edited (moved, renamed, given a room). Now that repeating events are expanded, both copies were being shown, so the same meeting appeared two or three times.

Entries are now collapsed per title and day. When only one of the copies carries the "this occurrence was edited" marker, that copy wins — including when the time was moved, which is why the check cannot simply compare start times.

Keep your settings: this installer now asks whether to erase the device before flashing. Leave erase off — erasing wipes the WiFi credentials, city and league selection, and the board will come back up at its factory defaults (city "New York").
