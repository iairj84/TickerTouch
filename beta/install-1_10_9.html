TickerTouch 1.10.9 — Stocks Unstarved, Sports Faster

Version 1.10.9 — beta. Stable is 1.10.8.

Stocks was never being fetched at all. The serial log was the giveaway: no [Stocks] lines whatsoever — not a refused request, not an empty response, no attempt. The fetch loop runs one feed at a time and returns after each, and it stamped a feed's timer on the way into the fetch. A sports pass takes about 66 seconds across five leagues against a 60-second interval, so sports was due again the instant it finished, re-entered at the top of the queue every time, and the stocks check below it was never reached. Two providers were never the problem; the fetch never ran.

Timers are now stamped after the fetch returns, which measures the interval from completion. A slow feed yields to the ones behind it instead of monopolising the loop.

Sports also spent each league's full timeout doing nothing. When a league's body ended, the parser looked for the next "competitors" block, found none, and simply refilled the buffer until the 12-second deadline expired — so every pass burned ~12s per league even though the data had already arrived. It now stops as soon as the connection closes with nothing left to parse, so a pass should complete in a few seconds instead of a minute.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off.

What to check
  • [Stocks] lines appear within about thirty seconds of boot — four of them, or three plus a no data line.
  • The sports pass finishes in a few seconds: [Sports] total=N follows the last league almost immediately instead of a minute later.
  • /diag shows every feed with a recent age, and stocks is no longer the one that never updates.
  • Finance still falls back to CNBC if Yahoo returns nothing — watch for (cnbc) after a price.
