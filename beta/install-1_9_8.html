TickerTouch — Rankings Get Their Own Column

Version 1.9.8 — beta. Stable is 1.9.4.

#8 was sitting on top of the badge. The rank label was pinned to the row's top edge at y=1 while the team badge starts at y=11, so the two overlapped and the number read as a smudge on the badge's rounded corner rather than as a rank.

Each side now has a 24 px gutter in the row margin, with the rank centred in it and vertically aligned to the badge. Badges moved inward by 20 px to make room, and the scores moved with them, so ranked and unranked teams still line up in the same columns.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off — erasing wipes WiFi, city, leagues, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

What to check after installing
  • On a ranked CFB or CBB game, the rank sits in the left margin, clear of the badge.
  • The away rank is left of the away badge, the home rank right of the home badge.
  • Unranked games keep their badges in the same place as ranked ones.
  • Scores, the centre status and the live dot are untouched.

Layout only: no change to how ranks are parsed, filtered or shown in the ticker.
