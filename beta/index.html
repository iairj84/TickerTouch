1.10.9 — Stocks Unstarved, Sports Faster

Version 1.10.9 — beta. Stable is 1.10.8.

Stocks was never being fetched at all. The serial log showed no [Stocks] lines — no attempt, not a refusal. The fetch loop runs one feed at a time, and it stamped each feed's timer on the way into the fetch, so a ~66-second sports pass against a 60-second interval was due again the instant it finished and the stocks check below it was never reached. Timers are now stamped after the fetch, so a slow feed yields to the ones behind it.

Sports also burned each league's full 12-second timeout after the body had already ended; it now stops as soon as the connection closes with nothing left to parse, so a pass takes seconds instead of a minute.

What to check: [Stocks] lines appear within about thirty seconds of boot, [Sports] total=N follows the last league almost immediately, and /diag shows every feed with a recent age. Full notes on the installer page.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off.
