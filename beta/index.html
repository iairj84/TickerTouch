TickerTouch 1.10.5 — The Missing Reconnect

Version 1.10.5 — beta. Stable is 1.9.4.

Nothing ever reconnected. connectSTA() joined the network once at boot, and that was the only WiFi.begin() in the firmware — there was no event handler, no auto-reconnect, no retry anywhere. Lose the lease, reboot the router, or walk out of range, and the device had no path back short of a power cycle.

Worse, isConnected() was just WiFi.status() == WL_CONNECTED. An association that has lost its address still reports connected, so the firmware believed the network was fine while holding 0.0.0.0: the settings page had nothing to answer on, and every feed failed without anything looking wrong. Now isConnected() requires an actual address.

The web task now supervises the link — it runs whether or not there is one. Every ten seconds it checks for connected plus an address; if that fails it calls WiFi.reconnect(), and every sixth attempt (about a minute) it tears the station down and re-associates from the saved credentials. The captive portal is left alone.

What to check

  • Note the settings IP, then reboot your router (or unplug its WAN briefly).
  • Within about ten seconds the device is back and the settings page answers again — no power cycle.
  • Serial shows [WiFi] link down (status=… ip=0.0.0.0) - attempt 1 and then recovery.
  • Data resumes on its own after the link returns.

If the screen is still empty while WiFi is genuinely up, send the /diag page — memory, network and per-feed item/age/failed rows. That separates "fetches dying" from "UI cannot redraw".

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off.
