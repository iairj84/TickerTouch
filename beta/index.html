TickerTouch 1.10.4 — The Day Boundary

Version 1.10.4 — beta. Stable is 1.9.4.

Nothing in the refresh loop ever compared dates. Each feed runs on its own interval — 30 seconds for stocks, a minute for scores, fifteen for the calendar — and none of them knows that midnight went past. "Today" changed underneath them: the scoreboard's date, the calendar's today/tomorrow offsets, the count of rows already retired. A new local day is now noticed explicitly, and on the first tick after it the retired-row count is cleared and every feed is refetched immediately.

And a failed sports cycle no longer wipes the tab. The refresh installed its staging count unconditionally, so when every league request failed it committed an empty list. The display rebuilds on a fingerprint of the data, which means an empty list counts as a real change: the tab cleared and stayed clear. It now commits an empty list only when a league actually answered and reported no games — if every request failed, the last good scores stay on screen.

If it still goes blank overnight, read /diag before rebooting. The dashboard says nothing about why it emptied, and two very different causes look identical on the glass. Open http://<device-ip>/diag and send:

  • Memory — heap free, heap low-water, PSRAM free.
  • Network — WiFi up or down, signal.
  • Data feeds — item count, age, and failed count per feed.
"0 items · 5 failed" means the fetches are dying. "12 items · just now" with a blank screen means the data is fine and the UI cannot redraw it — most likely memory, and the low-water figure will say so.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off.

What to check
  • Leave it running across midnight; in the morning sports, stocks and calendar populate.
  • Serial prints [Data] new day (…) refreshing every feed at the boundary.
  • Boot is still solid after the 1.10.2 change — restart it two or three times.
  • Header clock still local, panel not dimming at boot.

Previous build still available: 1.10.3, 1.10.1.
