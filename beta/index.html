TickerTouch — Ranks In The Ticker

Version 1.9.9 — beta. Stable is 1.9.4.

The scrolling line now names the rank. College games showed CFB: TENN 24-17 UK Final while the Sports tab showed #8 and #23 beside the badges — the same game described two different ways on the same screen.

Ranked teams now read CFB: #8 TENN 24-17 #23 UK Final. Unranked teams are unchanged, and the per-game buffer was widened so a ranked away team, a ranked home team and a long status cannot truncate each other.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off — erasing wipes WiFi, city, leagues, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

What to check after installing
  • A ranked college game shows #8 TENN style entries in the ticker.
  • Unranked games and pro leagues read exactly as before.
  • The lockup matches the Sports tab for the same game.
  • A full slate still scrolls to the end of the sports segment.

Display only: the rank was already parsed and already used by the Top 25 filter, so nothing about fetching or filtering changed.
