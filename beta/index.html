TickerTouch — The Setup Page Shows Your Settings

Version 1.10.1 — beta. Stable is 1.9.4.

1.10.0 stopped part of this and missed the real shape of it. The setup page was saved from a form carrying hard-coded defaults — stocks pre-filled with SPY,AAPL,TSLA,NVDA, a theme list with nothing marked selected (so Dark won), and NBA/NHL/MLB pre-ticked. Any visit to that page wrote all three over the saved settings.

Guarding the handler only on "first setup" could not work, because a missing SSID is exactly what sends the board to that page in the first place. The page is now built from the saved configuration: your stocks, your theme, your leagues, your city. Submitting it without editing anything writes back what is already stored.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off — erasing wipes WiFi, city, leagues, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

What changed
  • Stocks, theme and leagues on the setup page start from the saved values.
  • City and state come pre-filled too.
  • Coordinates are only invalidated when the city actually changes, so saving WiFi no longer forces a re-geocode.
  • A fresh device still gets sensible defaults, since that is what is stored.

Anything already overwritten by an earlier portal visit has to be set once in Settings — this build stops it recurring, it cannot recover the lost values.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.
