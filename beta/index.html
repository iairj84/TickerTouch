TickerTouch 1.10.3 — Timezone Without The Panic

Version 1.10.3 — beta. Stable is 1.9.4.

1.10.2 is withdrawn. It installed the saved timezone during setup(), which runs on Arduino's 8 KB loopTask stack. setenv() and tzset() parse a timezone rule table, and that stack is exactly why all the TLS and setup work lives in its own task. The result was a panic loop on the second boot — after the first boot had saved an offset — with no WiFi and nothing loading. Its installer page and manifest are gone from this site.

The same fix, moved to a stack that can hold it. The zone is now applied at the top of mainTask (40 KB, the task that has always called setenv()/tzset() from the timezone lookup) before anything touches localtime(). The header clock is repainted immediately afterwards, so it does not hold a UTC string until its own ten-second timer comes round.

Separately, the night window now refuses to evaluate until a zone actually exists (TZ is set), because comparing a UTC hour against your local window is what dimmed the panel at boot in the first place. That check is cheap and self-healing: the first 20-second brightness tick after the zone lands uses local time.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off. If 1.10.2 is panicking and you cannot reach this page from the device's own web server, that does not matter — this installer talks to the board over USB.

What to check
  • It boots repeatedly — especially the third boot, not just the first.
  • Serial shows [TZ] boot: … from the Main task, then [Boot] WiFi:.
  • Header clock reads local time; on reboot no UTC flash.
  • Panel does not dim at boot unless it is genuinely inside your night window.
  • Dashboard loads: sports, calendar, stocks all populate.

Still on 1.10.1 and want to stay there? That installer still works. Please send the serial log if anything goes wrong — the panic backtrace identifies the failing task far faster than guessing does.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.
