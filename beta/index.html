TickerTouch — Timezone Control

Version 1.7.0 — this is not the default firmware.

The clock and the calendar take their timezone from an IP lookup. When that lookup is wrong — a VPN, a phone hotspot, an address the database maps to another state — the whole device sits in the wrong zone. After a daylight-saving change that shows up as everything being an hour out, while entries the feed sends as plain local time still look right.

Settings now has a Timezone dropdown, so the zone can be set directly instead of hoped for. /diag also gained a Time card that states the zone in use, the offset it is applying, and what the clock and UTC each read.

Keep your settings: this installer asks whether to erase the device before flashing. Leave erase off — erasing wipes the WiFi credentials, city and league selection, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

What to check after installing
  • Open /diag and read the Time card.
  • If Zone is not yours, set it in Settings → Display → Timezone.
  • Restart from the settings page, then re-check the Time card.
  • Calendar entries and the header clock should now agree with your phone.

A wrong zone and a wrong conversion look identical from the outside, which is why the page reports what the device is actually applying rather than what it was told. If the zone is right and entries are still out, that narrows it to the feed's own timestamps.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.
