1.10.6 — Surviving Midnight

Version 1.10.6 — beta. Stable is 1.10.5.

Stable 1.10.5 stops fetching data at local midnight — scores, calendar, stocks, crypto and weather all freeze on the evening's last fetch, and the calendar keeps claiming Today with yesterday's date. Its day-rollover check never records the new day, so every tick re-arms the timers and returns without fetching. Rebooting clears it until the next midnight. 1.10.6 advances the marker before returning, so the boundary is handled once and fetching continues.

What to check: the dashboard loads normally now; after the next local midnight /diag shows recent ages for every feed instead of everything frozen at 23:5x, and the calendar header names the actual date. Full notes on the installer page.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off.
