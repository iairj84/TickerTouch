TickerTouch — One Expander For Sports Filter

Version 1.9.7 — beta. Stable is 1.9.4.

Sports Filter had two expanders. When the settings page gained collapsible sections, the script looked for each section's heading and wrapped everything below it — but Sports Filter already had its own heading with a ▼ expand link. The result was a section header stacked on top of the old one, with the old "expand" link left stranded and unclickable.

That inner toggle is gone. Sports Filter now behaves like every other section: the header opens and closes it, and the explanation, team pickers and league groups are all visible once it is open. The per-league groups inside it still fold independently, which is what they are for.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off — erasing wipes WiFi, city, leagues, both calendar URLs and the icon choices.

What to check after installing
  • Sports Filter has one heading, and clicking it opens the section.
  • No stray "▼ expand" line appears under the heading.
  • The league groups inside still fold on their own.
  • If you already have a team filter saved, that section starts open.
  • Saving still keeps the filter — it is submitted with the form as before.

Purely a settings-page fix: no firmware behaviour, feed parsing or saved keys changed.

Your browser does not support Web Serial — use Chrome or Edge on desktop. Web Serial needs HTTPS (this page is) and a direct connection.
