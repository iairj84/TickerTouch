TickerTouch — Hardened Test Build

Version 1.4.0 — this is not the default firmware.

Photos are now composed at exactly the panel size (480×272) instead of 520×300, so the screensaver draws them one-for-one with no scaling and no blur strips along the edges.

Any photo whose shape is within about a third of the panel's is centre-cropped to fill the frame instead of being letterboxed in blurred edges — that covers normal landscape shots, including 4:3 phone and camera images. Portrait and square photos, which would lose more than half of themselves to a crop, keep the whole picture with the blurred surround.

Photos uploaded before this build are still cropped into their files. Remove them and upload again to get the full, uncropped framing.

Keep your settings: this installer now asks whether to erase the device before flashing. Leave erase off — erasing wipes the WiFi credentials, city and league selection, and the board will come back up at its factory defaults (city "New York").
