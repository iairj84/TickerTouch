TickerTouch 1.10.6 — Surviving Midnight

Version 1.10.6 — beta. Stable is 1.10.5.

1.10.5 stops fetching data at local midnight. Its day-rollover check compares the current day-of-year against the last one it saw, and when they differ it clears the retired-row count, re-arms every feed timer and returns — but it never records the new day before returning. So the comparison stays true, and from then on every one-second tick re-arms the timers and returns before the fetch dispatch runs. Nothing is fetched again until a reboot.

On screen that reads as yesterday frozen in place: scores still showing FINAL from last night, the calendar header saying Today - Nov 10 because its rows still carry yesterday's dayOffset, and every feed on /diag stuck at waiting with its last-success age climbing. The clock and the settings page keep working, because they are different tasks, and the watchdog never trips, because the loop is still spinning normally. Only the data dies.

The fix is one assignment moved before the return. The day marker advances first, then the refresh happens, so the boundary is noticed exactly once per day and the fetch loop picks up immediately afterwards. Nothing else about the rollover changes: the retired-row count still resets, and every feed still refetches once under the new date instead of waiting out its own interval.

Keep your settings: the installer asks whether to erase the device. Leave erase off.

What to check
  • Immediately: the dashboard loads as before — sports, calendar, stocks, crypto.
  • After the next local midnight: /diag shows every feed with a recent age and no waiting, instead of everything frozen at 23:5x.
  • The calendar header reads Today - <the actual date> in the morning, and yesterday's games are gone from the scoreboard.
  • Serial shows one [Data] new day (…): refreshing every feed line at the boundary — one, not one per second.

Still on 1.10.5? It keeps working until the next midnight. Rebooting it clears the wedge and feeds resume, but they will stop again at the following midnight.

Previous build: 1.10.5, 1.10.4, 1.10.3, 1.10.1.
