TickerTouch — Hardened Test Build

Version 1.5.5 — this is not the default firmware.

Fixes a monthly meeting appearing every week.

A "second Tuesday of the month" style rule is written as BYDAY=2TU. Only the weekday was being read and the number was discarded, so the rule matched every Tuesday — the meeting turned up on the first Tuesday, the third and the fourth as well.

The occurrence number is now honoured, including last-of-month rules such as -1FR. /cal also gained a Repeating series section that lists every block carrying a rule, with the rule text, since a series created months ago sits too far back in the feed to appear in the recent-events trace.

Keep your settings: this installer now asks whether to erase the device before flashing. Leave erase off — erasing wipes the WiFi credentials, city and league selection, and the board will come back up at its factory defaults (city "New York").
